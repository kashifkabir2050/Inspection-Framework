🔧 Firebase Configuration

Enter your Firebase project details once — they'll be saved in the cloud so any device or browser connects automatically next time. Find these in Firebase Console → Project Settings → General → Your apps.

One-time setup: After connecting, your config is saved to Firestore. On other devices, just enter your Project ID using the Quick Connect box below — no need to re-enter everything.

First-time setup: After connecting Firebase, the app will automatically create the 6 SPEA standards structure and an admin account (admin@spea.ae / admin123456). You can change these credentials after logging in.

Other devices: Use the Quick Connect box — just enter your Firebase Project ID and everything loads automatically.
